# Asset credits

## Technology marks

The rounded icon tiles in `toolbox.svg` use [Skill Icons](https://github.com/tandpfun/skill-icons) (MIT, license below). Additional technology symbols come from [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons) (CC0): Expo and Jetpack Compose.

ChromaDB uses [Chroma's official wordmark](https://github.com/chroma-core/chroma/blob/main/docs/assets/chroma-wordmark-color.png), with its symbol displayed inside a clipped viewport. Computer Vision uses an original concept symbol.

The parrot-style LangChain and LangGraph marks come from [Lobe Icons](https://github.com/lobehub/lobe-icons), MIT licensed. [LangChain source](https://github.com/lobehub/lobe-icons/blob/298dcb34e1109e97a1713fc2b9f3a7aebea8d483/packages/static-svg/icons/langchain.svg); [LangGraph source](https://github.com/lobehub/lobe-icons/blob/master/packages/static-svg/icons/langgraph.svg). Their monochrome marks use mint on dark green for contrast.

Brand names and marks belong to their respective owners and identify technologies used by Lokith.
